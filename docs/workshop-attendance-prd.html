01 Purpose & goals

Workshop organizers need a reliable student roster and consistent attendance records. Spreadsheets make it difficult to see missing records, calculate time spent, and identify students who miss sessions.

Make attendance simple

Find a session quickly, see its students, and mark each student as Unmarked, Present, or Absent.

Make participation visible

Review recorded workshop time, compare attendance across sessions, and export usable reports.

Basis of this draft

Based on your feature sketch: an admin portal, student list, attendance marking, calendar, time spent, analytics graphs, and reporting. Product choices added to make these features implementable are proposals pending confirmation.

03 Core workflow

→→→→
Step 1 · Admin · Set up

Create a workshop and enroll students with a name and unique identifier. The roster defines who can have attendance recorded.

Step 2 · Admin · Schedule

Create a session with a date, start time, end time, and time zone. Each session appears in the calendar.

Step 3 · Facilitator · Open

Select a session in the calendar. Its enrolled students appear with attendance initially Unmarked.

Step 4 · Facilitator · Record

Mark attendance and enter participation times where available. Save feedback identifies pending changes, successful saves, and failures that need a retry.

Step 5 · Admin · Review

Review totals, incomplete attendance, and recorded hours. Export filtered reports or correct records when required.

05 Attendance recording

StatusMeaning
UnmarkedNo attendance decision has been recorded.
PresentThe student attended the session.
AbsentThe student did not attend the session.
  • ATT-01: Maintain one attendance status per enrolled student per session, initially Unmarked.
  • ATT-02: Support individual status changes, corrections to saved attendance, and resetting a status to Unmarked.
  • ATT-03: Search by name or identifier and filter by attendance status.
  • ATT-04: Display Present, Absent, and Unmarked counts for the selected session.
  • ATT-05: Support bulk marking, including “Mark all present,” with a clearly stated target group.
  • ATT-06: Show saving, saved, and error feedback. Failed saves preserve the selected values and offer a retry.
Data invariant

Unmarked remains distinct from Absent. The app must never silently convert an incomplete record into an absence.

06 Time spent in the workshop

Proposed V1 approach: facilitators manually enter a student's check-in and check-out times. Automatic tracking is a later enhancement.

→→→
Step 1 · Record arrival

Enter a student's arrival time for the selected session. Attendance status remains a separate field.

Step 2 · Record departure

Enter the departure time. A missing departure is flagged as incomplete; the system does not invent a duration.

Step 3 · Validate timestamps

Reject departure times earlier than arrival times. Authorized staff can correct timestamps, with the change recorded in the audit history.

Step 4 · Calculate participation

Participation minutes equal check-out minus check-in. Show minutes for each session and sum valid durations into total student hours.

1 / 4
Missing data is visible

Present does not imply full-session participation. Missing time data displays as “Not recorded” or “Incomplete,” rather than zero. Example: 10:15 to 11:45 equals 90 recorded minutes.

07 Analytics graphs & reporting

Dashboard measureDefinition / presentation
Total enrolled studentsStudent count for the selected workshop.
Session attendancePresent, Absent, and Unmarked counts, with an incomplete-record indicator.
Attendance ratePresent ÷ (Present + Absent) × 100. Display “No data” when the denominator is zero.
Attendance trend graphAttendance rate across sessions, with session dates and counts available to the reader.
Participation hoursSum of valid recorded durations. Display incomplete time-record counts alongside totals.
Repeated absencesStudent absence counts for the chosen date range. A low-attendance threshold is still to be defined.
Interpretation rule

Attendance rate excludes Unmarked records, so every rate must show how many records remain Unmarked. Example: 8 Present, 2 Absent, and 5 Unmarked gives 80% attendance among marked records, with 5 records still incomplete.

Required export fields

08 Quality requirements

  • Access control: only authorized staff can view or modify student and attendance data. Permissions must be enforced for every protected action.
  • Reliability: saved records persist after refresh and appear consistently on another device.
  • Data integrity: prevent duplicate student-session attendance records. Historical attendance survives student removal or archiving.
  • Auditability: record who changed attendance or participation times and when.
  • Accessibility: support keyboard navigation, readable labels, and attendance states distinguishable without relying on color.
  • Responsive layout: core roster, calendar, and attendance actions work on desktop and mobile screens.
  • Time handling: store timestamps consistently and display them using the workshop's configured time zone.
  • Error recovery: preserve unsaved input after a failed save and provide a clear retry action.

09 Release criteria & success measures

Acceptance checklist

  • An admin can add, edit, enroll, remove, and archive students; duplicate identifiers are rejected.
  • An admin can create sessions and open each session from the calendar, including separate sessions on the same day.
  • Attendance starts Unmarked and can be marked Present or Absent, corrected, or reset.
  • Individual and bulk attendance changes save correctly; saved values survive a page refresh.
  • Removing or archiving students preserves previous attendance records.
  • Valid timestamps produce the correct duration; invalid or incomplete timestamps are identified.
  • Dashboard counts and attendance rates match the underlying records, including “No data” cases.
  • CSV exports match selected filters and preserve identifiable missing time data.
  • Unauthorized access is rejected, and authorized changes record the staff member and timestamp.
  • Core screens work on mobile and support keyboard access.
